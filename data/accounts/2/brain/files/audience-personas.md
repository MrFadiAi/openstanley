# Target Audience (refined 2026-09-01)

Primary: Arabic-speaking builders, roughly 20-35, mostly Iraqi/Gulf dialect, who follow English AI Twitter but never get the layer they actually need — what tools cost, what to click, and how to make the first $100.
Secondary: Vibe coders and non-technical builders using Claude Code, Browser Use, and local models — they don't want model news, they want workflows, tool verdicts, and token/cost numbers from someone who actually ran the thing.
Others: Small Arabic creators who want to make AI video, streams, and content — they'll watch the video and buy the course, but only from someone who shows the receipts first.
