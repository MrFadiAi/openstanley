# Learned Rules

Numbered DO/DON'T rules. Each rule carries its source (chat | learn | scan)
and the date it was learned. Retired rules stay listed but struck through.

## [R1] (chat · 2026-08-19 · active)
DO consider bilingual (EN/AR) posts when the user writes a request in Arabic

## [R2] (chat · 2026-08-19 · active)
DON'T assume timezone for absolute times like '9:00' — ask or default to user's known tz

## [R3] (chat · 2026-08-19 · active)
DO keep hooks concrete — numbers beat adjectives (verified in browser test)

## [R99] (chat · 2026-08-19 · retired)

## [R100] (chat · 2026-08-19 · active)

## [R101] (scan · 2026-08-19 · active)

## [R102] (scan · 2026-08-19 · active)

## [R103] (scan · 2026-08-19 · active)
